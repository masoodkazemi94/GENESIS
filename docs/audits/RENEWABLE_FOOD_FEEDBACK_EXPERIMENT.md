# Shared renewable-food feedback experiment

## Source finding and candidate

Model 1's mating gate divides route-open standing plants and carrion by nearby same-diet animals. Model 2 divides standing plants by all nearby noncarnivores and carrion by same-diet animals. Feeding lets herbivores, scavengers, and omnivores use the same plant patches; scavengers can also eat carrion at a wider foraging range. The gate checks energy, hydration, maturity, age, and cooldown before local food. Plant growth is climate-, terrain-, stock-, and capacity-dependent. The gate does not project that growth over the 180-tick-plus-gestation cooldown, and its proximity-only denominator can include consumers across a blocked route. These are measurable limitations, not proof that current feedback is inadequate.

The isolated, unsaved `renewableFoodFeedback` development option applies only to Model 2. For each eligible noncarnivore it sums route-open plant stock once and current potential plant growth times that parent's cooldown interval once, then divides by unique nearby route-connected plant eaters. Scavengers add route-open carrion stock divided by route-connected scavengers. It retains the existing 16-unit mating threshold and every energy, hydration, age, cooldown, mate, and cap check. It does not change plant growth, feeding, metabolism, reproduction costs, or prey. The spatial index limits the queried patches and consumers; a per-decision-pass map caches patch growth. It performs one route check per local patch or local consumer, never a route check for every resource-consumer pair. Current climate and growth are held constant in the projection, so this is an estimate, not a guaranteed future harvest. Patches absent from the food index at amount ≤1 remain conservatively omitted.

Before the paired run, the acceptance cost bound is **at most 1.5× headless elapsed time and 2× route checks for each seed**, with no severe new cap occupancy or systematic multi-role collapse. Seed outcomes must be considered separately; no retuning will be done on these seeds. The initial horizon is 10,000 ticks. Both conditions start from the same serialized Model 2 founders, terrain, resources, and RNG state; the switch is absent from saves. Experimental litter and predator placement remain off.

## Controlled layouts

`src/renewable_food_feedback.test.ts` runs five deterministic, flat-terrain layouts against both ordinary and candidate Model 2 gating. A: high standing stock and negligible growth retains eligibility in both. B: low standing stock and strong growth is ineligible in ordinary Model 2 and eligible under the candidate; the actual growth rule also replenishes more than 16 biomass units during 180 ticks of repeated 0.2-unit grazing. C: one herbivore and one scavenger share a plant patch, with two unique plant consumers and ineligibility in both. D: a scavenger with scarce plants and accessible carrion is candidate-eligible, with carrion counted separately. E: a wall blocks a high-stock, high-growth patch and a scavenger; the candidate counts neither and remains ineligible. These tests establish gate behavior only; the screen below evaluates ecological consequences.

## Paired screen

`scripts/renewable_food_feedback_experiment.ts` ran the four specified seeds for 10,000 ticks each. Each pair began from an identical full save, including PRNG state. Windows of 250 ticks and observer counters are in the [raw JSON](data/renewable_food_feedback_results.json). The same-seed outcomes are development evidence, not an independent holdout. Counts below are ordinary Model 2 → candidate. Final roles abbreviate herbivore H, scavenger S, omnivore O; carnivores were zero at the end of every run. The candidate made more organisms food-eligible in each seed, but did not establish multi-role persistence. Herbivores were absent or down to one at the end of three candidate runs; scavengers dominated all nonextinct worlds. Ordinary Model 2 had one carnivore birth on 691153, while the candidate had none there; no carnivore survived in either condition. No predator reproductive replacement was observed. Local reachable standing plant per consumer and below-threshold consumer counts are stored per window. Candidate below-16 sample incidences were 3,255/5,088, 1,356/2,444, 2,976/4,582, and 3,266/5,639 respectively, versus 2,474/4,606, 1,248/2,402, 2,734/4,596, and 2,621/5,231 in ordinary Model 2. These samples include immature consumers and are not direct mating decisions. All eight runs depleted plant biomass to a sampled trough and later recovered some biomass; 260679 remained extinct after recovery. The window series records those troughs and recoveries.

Headless elapsed ratios ranged 1.02–1.26×, and route-check ratios 1.14–1.34×. Spatial organism-query calls were recorded for each run in JSON. Both cost bounds passed. The cap-prone seed is the clear failure: 594 ticks at the safety ceiling and 2,221 blocked mating attempts appeared under the candidate, versus zero for ordinary Model 2. These are blocked attempts, not 2,221 distinct prevented offspring. The candidate increased births and cap pressure while scavenger dominance and predator loss persisted. Fewer or more births alone would not establish ecological balance.

## Decision and verification

**Reject the candidate.** The controlled mechanism responds to renewable growth, but the paired screen fails the cap-interference and multi-role persistence gates. No parameter retuning or larger holdout was run. The option remains unsaved and development-only; Models 0–2 and the Model 0 default are unchanged. The next architecture priority is condition-dependent juvenile and birth investment, subject to its own controlled evidence.

The full suite passed 112/112 tests. TypeScript, build, and the unchanged version-2 determinism baseline passed; its nine Model 0–2 trajectories repeated and restored through tick 2,000 without overwriting archived hashes. The candidate repeated exactly on seed 691153 through tick 2,000 and continued identically after an explicitly re-enabled save. Each paired run also checked a 20-tick save continuation from tick 1,000. An old-save compatibility check is included in the version-2 baseline verifier. This confirms the tested horizons, not indefinite replay or browser performance.
