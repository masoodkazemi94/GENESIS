# GENESIS development backlog

## Roadmap status

The three-task architecture roadmap is **closed**. Predator encounter placement failed its multi-generation gate; shared renewable-food feedback failed the cap-interference gate; the [juvenile investment experiment](docs/audits/JUVENILE_INVESTMENT_EXPERIMENT.md) failed its controlled reproductive-fitness gate. Its unsaved Model 1 gate withheld nine no-food maturity labels across seeds 691153, 260679, 10101, but did not change fed focal death or first successful reproduction in 135 cloned branches. Eight no-food focal deaths occurred in either condition. Combined development plus litter and the 5,000-tick population screen were not run. No experimental mechanic is approved for adoption. The next phase is a coordinated redesign proposal covering predator income and mate access, shared renewable resource flow, and paid offspring investment with persistent save/replay state; define multi-role lineage gates before implementation. Do not retune the rejected candidates on reused seeds.

## Completed task history

The [predator capture experiment](docs/audits/PREDATOR_CAPTURE_EXPERIMENT.md) rejected a bounded wounded-prey follow-through rule: 22 paired post-strike encounters on three reused development seeds showed identical captures (22/22), strikes (26), and net energy (1,381.263) in baseline and candidate. No candidate population screen or holdout was justified. The unsaved Model 1 probe remains isolated and is not approved for adoption. Predator lineage replacement remains unproven.

The [predator encounter-placement screen](docs/audits/PREDATOR_PLACEMENT_EXPERIMENT.md) is complete and failed its multi-generation success gate. The development-only Model 1 switch remains isolated; no candidate is approved for production. Keep Model 0 as default and use the review's multi-role scorecard for subsequent candidates.

The [determinism baseline audit](docs/audits/DETERMINISM_BASELINE_AUDIT.md) resolved the archived hash mismatch: commit `054e9e1` intentionally corrected the saved reproduction-tax ledger. Version 2 references now cover Models 0–2 on three development seeds through 2,000 ticks; fresh repeat and save/restore comparisons pass. Use this baseline as the reproducibility gate for the next ecology experiment, and version any later intentional trajectory change.

The [shared renewable-food feedback screen](docs/audits/RENEWABLE_FOOD_FEEDBACK_EXPERIMENT.md) completed the second architecture priority and rejected its unsaved Model 2 candidate. All five controlled layouts passed, but seed 166064 had 594 cap ticks and 2,221 blocked mating attempts versus zero in its baseline, while predator persistence and broad herbivore persistence did not improve. No retuning or holdout is planned for this candidate. Move to the third architecture priority, condition-dependent juvenile and birth investment.

## Critical blockers

- Carnivores do not persist to tick 10,000 in reported development seed sets. The three-seed placement screen improved mate access but left predator net energy negative and produced no reproducing descendants by tick 5,000. A higher hunt-return intervention reached generation 5 in only one earlier seed. General viability remains unproven.
- Earlier 50,000-tick experimental survivors often touched the 220-organism cap. The new 10,000/30,000-tick runs showed no sustained cap saturation or blocked births, so the longer-run mechanism remains unresolved.
- No completed benchmark demonstrates population-level speciation or supports promotion of an experimental model.
- Cloned no-food juveniles all crossed the age-only maturity threshold; lower reserves led to earlier starvation afterward, with no adult reproduction. The litter screen still lacks predator-lineage persistence and touched the cap in one seed.

## Closed three-task roadmap

1. **Completed diagnostic; further predator economics deferred to redesign.** Wounded-prey follow-through changed no measured outcome in 22 matched post-strike encounters. No profitable accepted hunts lost after contact were demonstrated. Any future finite post-kill allocation must preserve carrion and mass accounting; do not retune placement or follow-through on the reused seeds or relax rejected-prey scoring by default.
2. **Completed and rejected: shared renewable-food feedback.** The isolated Model 2 candidate increased eligibility but created cap interference on seed 166064 without improving multi-role persistence. Keep it development-only and do not retune on the screened seeds.
3. **Completed and rejected: condition-dependent juvenile development.** A measured-intake, reserve, and health gate separated chronological age from physiological maturity only in no-food branches and produced no measured reproductive-fitness change. Litter v1 remains unadopted; the combined treatment and population screen did not pass the staged gate.

## Deferred

Population-level speciation implementation until spatially distinct reproducing lineages can be evaluated; major ecology rewrites, new rendering features, and additional long-running benchmarks without a screened candidate. Any candidate needs independent-seed validation, runtime, cap, and deterministic save/replay gates before promotion.
